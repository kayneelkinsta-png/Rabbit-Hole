      What is

      Rabbit Hole

      Doom scrolling? Never heard of it. This is falling down a rabbit hole, on purpose.

      • WorldsEvery level of the rabbit hole is a world, made of one topic and all the topics connected to it. Pick any orb, enter its world, and you're in a whole new world.
      • OrbsEach orb is a real article that connects to the world you are in. Tap an orb to reveal its tile.
      • Wander or playFall for hours, or take on the Wikipedia challenge: reach a hidden goal in as few clicks as you can.

      the rabbit hole game

      1. You start in one world, and your goal is hidden in another.
      2. Every orb is a link to another world. Click one and you jump into it.
      3. Each jump uses up one of your clicks, so choose carefully.
      4. Spot your goal's orb and click it to win. There's no search, and climbing back up doesn't give clicks back.
